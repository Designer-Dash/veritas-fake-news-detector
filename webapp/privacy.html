Privacy by Design: Veritas Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Zero Storage Principle

Veritas is built on a zero-storage principle for all analysis endpoints. We do not store, persist, or sell your reading history. Analysis results are calculated in real-time and immediately discarded.

2. Data Minimization (On-Device Processing)

We process data locally on your device whenever possible. With our Data Minimization Update, Veritas extracts the core claim from an article directly inside your browser. Only the claim and the headline are sent to our servers for verification—the full article text, including private comments or paywalled content, never leaves your device.

3. Cryptographic Anonymization

When you use our service without an account, your IP address is never logged in plaintext. It is cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 for basic abuse prevention (rate-limiting) and discarded. Server logs only contain hashed URLs (e.g., example.com#a1b2c3d4e5f6) so that we can debug system issues without tracking what you read.

4. Open Source and Verifiable

Our extension and backend logic are fully transparent. You can inspect the network requests in your browser to verify that we only transmit the minimum data necessary for a fact-check.
